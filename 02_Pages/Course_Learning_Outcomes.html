What “piloted” means for AI-C

Piloted in Fall 2026. This objective is not yet part of the approved course description; it is being taught and assessed this year to determine whether it should be proposed as a formal course outcome. It is graded like any other objective in this section.

Every assignment page, rubric and session that invokes it says so, because a student graded against an objective they cannot find on the syllabus has a legitimate complaint — and the pilot's own results would be contaminated by the resentment.

What happens next. If the pilot works, it is proposed to the curriculum committee as a formal outcome. If it does not, it is withdrawn. Either way you will have been told what you were assessed on before you were assessed on it, which is the only part of this that is not negotiable.

How outcomes are reported

Course outcomes roll up to program outcomes for the GIST degree. A rubric criterion reports to a course outcome; a course outcome reports to a program outcome; program outcomes are what the degree is accredited on.
