GIS + AI Knowledge Check — Pre

Week 1 — diagnostic · about twenty minutes · no points

 

What this is, and why you are doing it

A self-assessment. You get your own results back with the answers explained, which is the point of doing it — most people find at least one of the scenario questions surprising.

Before any AI instruction in this course. Answer from what you do now, not what you think you should say.

Two parts, taken in one sitting

What to do
1. Knowledge CheckTwenty questions across six topics, running from straightforward to genuinely hard. They have right answers. Canvas grades it immediately and shows you why each answer is what it is — that explanation is the part worth your time.
2. Practice InventoryHow you actually work, how confident you feel, and one question asking you to predict your own score. Take it straight after the Knowledge Check and before you look at your result, or the prediction question measures nothing.

Do not look anything up

A diagnostic that flatters you is useless to both of us. Its whole job is to tell me where to pitch this course — answer it strategically and you get a term aimed at a class that does not exist. There are no points attached and it cannot affect your grade.

What happens to your answers

You get your own profile back by topic, with every answer explained. I use the aggregate — never individual results — to decide what to teach and at what level. You take the same check again in the last week, so you can see what changed.

Anonymity

Aggregate results are reported to the department and may appear in published work about how this course was designed. Anything reported is de-identified before anyone analyzes it, and no individual result is ever shown to anyone but you.
