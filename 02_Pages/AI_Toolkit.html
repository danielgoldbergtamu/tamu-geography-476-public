These are the instruments the whole design rests on, and both are graded.

The two logs
PageWhat it records
AI Review LogWhat you asked a reviewer, what it said, and what you rejected and why
AI Method LogWhere AI entered the technical work, and how you verified what it produced

The distinction matters: the Review Log is about judgment on writing and argument, the Method Log is about judgment on method and data. An assignment that requires one will say so.

The rule underneath all of it

You are assessed on how well you direct and evaluate these tools, never on whether you used one. Accepting every suggestion is not review — it is transcription, and it is visible in a log. The critiques you overruled, and why, are the most valuable thing you will write down.
