What does not exist

  • Coordinates for any screwworm case. Neither APHIS nor SENASICA publishes them, so nothing in this analysis draws a case as a point. Everything is an areal rate or a position on a one-dimensional axis.
  • Municipio-level CUMULATIVE counts. The Power BI report holds them and the matrix stops feeding after ten states, so what can be extracted is the active snapshot - 1,941 of 40,392 cases, five percent of the epidemic.
  • Any measure of surveillance effort by location. The sterile-release objection could be tested because the case list records releases; "somebody looked here harder" cannot be tested at all.

Where the sources disagreed

Reported rather than resolved. When two sources disagree and neither is obviously wrong, the disagreement is part of the result.

  • Does the thinning of cases toward the border reflect the outbreak or the response to it?
  • The intervention reading: USDA releases sterile flies at the border first, so the near-border deficit is the program working.
  • The data: cases with a release sit at a median 82 miles out, cases without at 81, KS p 0.355. The release pattern does not explain the gradient.
  • What remains unresolved: surveillance EFFORT by distance is not published at all, so "somebody looked harder near the border" cannot be tested and is not claimed either way.

What was NOT built, and why

Read this section first if you read nothing else. The deliverable that was refused is the one you would most likely have produced.

  • Using the municipio counts that were successfully extracted. They exist only for the ten states with the most active cases. Any correlation against them would have been guaranteed and circular.
  • Drawing the cases as points anywhere on the figure. No case coordinate is published. A dot on a map is a claim about a place, and every one of them would have been invented.

What no check would have caught

The rules and gates this course runs on, and why each one would have passed the version that was wrong.

  • any checker here - a correlation run against a sample selected on the response variable produces a beautiful result and a green build
  • the p-value - the Monte Carlo difference is significant at p 0.001 and is 1.65 km, which is to say significant and meaningless at once

What went wrong while building it

  • Municipio-level counts were extracted and then excluded from every result. The matrix is sorted by descending active cases, so the ten states it gave up are the ten states with the most cases - a sample selected on the response variable. Caught by Asking why exactly ten states came out, instead of being pleased that ten had..
  • The statistics panel on the figure ran off the bottom of its box. Fixed leading, chosen before the content was measured. Caught by Looking at the render. Third occurrence of the same mistake in this repository, after the evidence slide twice..
  • The first American test reported a 127 km market effect that was entirely a border confound. The null was weighted by cattle, which was the confounder the Mexican analysis had been built to defeat - and the American geography carries a second one the Mexican geography does not. Detections sit 110 km from the border, Texas cattle sit 453 km, and no approved market lies within 129 miles of the river east of El Paso. Caught by Asking what else is true of the counties where the detections landed, and then running the test again with border distance held constant..
  • Two reference lines on the Texas bar chart were captioned in text laid across the bars they were meant to be compared against. Labeling a line where the line is rather than where there is room. Caught by Looking at the render. Replaced with a legend..
  • A reproducibility check failed on one of three derived files and then would not recur. The American-English gate had run in between and corrected "neighbor_pairs" to "neighbor_pairs" - a dictionary key inside the generator that is written straight into the output. One byte shorter. Every number was identical; only a label had moved. Caught by Chasing a one-byte difference instead of re-running until it passed..

What to take from it

  • Put the confounder in the NULL, not in the discussion. Cases and markets are both where the cattle are, so a uniform-random null is one that any spatial pattern beats. Weighting every draw by the cattle surface turns an unfalsifiable test into a real one.
  • A null result is only worth printing if the test could have found something. Show the null model on the figure, and show a pattern the same method DOES detect - here, Moran's I at 0.448 - so the absence is believable rather than merely unproven.
  • Match the analysis to the resolution of the data. Incidence exists as state totals and as distances from a line. Neither is a point, so nothing was drawn as a point.
  • Restrict every distribution to the same frame before comparing them. The case list is truncated at 100 miles; comparing it against an untruncated cattle distribution would have manufactured the result.
  • Test the obvious objection instead of arguing with it. "The border is thin because they drop sterile flies there" was answerable from a column already in the file: p 0.355, no difference.
  • Report effect size beside significance. With 398 cases a 1.65 km difference reaches p 0.001, and 1.65 km means nothing on a map of northern Mexico.
  • A dataset selected on the response variable is worse than no dataset, because it produces a confident answer. Ten states chosen for having the most cases cannot be used to ask what causes cases.
  • Say which files on disk did NOT contribute to the result. A file sitting in a data directory implies it was used unless the record says otherwise.
  • A null model corrects for the confounders you put IN it, and only those. Weighting the American null by cattle removed the cattle confound and left the border confound standing - and the border confound was worth 127 km. After every test, ask again: what else is true of the places where the events happened?
  • Report the smallest effect a test could have seen. With 13 counties, "no significant difference" and "we could not have detected one" are different sentences, and only one of them is a result.
  • A prose-fixing gate can rewrite a dictionary key inside a generator and change the schema of what it emits. After any such gate runs, a rebuild is comparing two different programs rather than two runs of one - which is how a spelling correction produced a failed checksum on a file whose every number was unchanged.

What this means for the job you are about to apply for

The client asked whether the aggregation points explain the cases. The answer is no, and the useful version of that answer is a change of timing rather than a shrug: the epidemic is currently a front, the facilities sit ahead of it, and aggregation is the mechanism that turns a front into a jump. Being able to deliver a negative result that still changes the plan is most of what separates an analyst from a person who runs tests. The instructor then asked why the analysis had been run on Mexico only, which was the right question and is answered in the log's addendum: two of the three tests do not transfer to 47 detections, the third does, and it produced the most instructive result in either country.
