What does not exist

  • Throughput at an American livestock auction market. Nobody publishes it. This is the reason the ranking is built on convergence instead: a ranking by throughput would have silently deleted every market that does not report to USDA AMS, and the deletion would have looked like a finding.
  • A coordinate on either facility register. Both publish a street address and neither publishes a point, and no geocoding service is reachable from this project - so every coordinate here is a join against a published place file, and each facility records which pass located it.
  • A Mexican equivalent of the APHIS approval concept. The Mexican list is of slaughter plants, not of markets approved for movement, so the two sides of the map are different instruments and no number crosses between them.

Where the sources disagreed

Reported rather than resolved. When two sources disagree and neither is obviously wrong, the disagreement is part of the result.

  • How many cattle a year pass through Mexico's slaughter facilities?
  • The SENASICA facility list: 634 plants with a readable monthly cattle figure, totaling 222,102 head a month - about 2.7 million a year.
  • SIAP's municipal production returns: 12,541,720 head slaughtered in 2025, roughly five times as many.
  • Both numbers are printed on the slide and the gap is named. Ninety- three plants have capacity cells too ambiguous to parse, which accounts for part of it but nowhere near five-fold. The facility list is visibly incomplete, and its own title says it covers what the state governments know about.

What was NOT built, and why

Read this section first if you read nothing else. The deliverable that was refused is the one you would most likely have produced.

  • A single ranking of markets and rastros together. Approval for interstate movement and known-to-the-state-government are not the same category, and capacity in head is published on one side only. One ordered list would have implied a comparison that neither register supports.
  • Apportioning a bare capacity number across a multi-species plant. "350" on a plant that takes cattle, pigs and sheep is genuinely ambiguous. Ninety-three plants are left blank rather than divided by a number nobody published.

What went wrong while building it

  • The country split was done with latitude rules, which handed every Mexican cell north of 25.9 N to American markets. Trying to follow a river that runs from 25.9 N to 31.8 N through deep bends with a few horizontal cuts. Caught by Reading the printed totals. 43.8 million head allocated to US markets from a window that stopped at 40.5 N was not obviously wrong until the Mexican number was read beside it..
  • Ninety-four British spellings had been introduced across three earlier logs and language_check had never been run. Never running the gate. Caught by Running the gate..
  • figs_plans.py still drew the sq03 specimen after sq03 was promoted, and would have done the same to sq05 - overwriting a real map with stand-in values that still carried the register's alt text saying it was real. Promoting a plan touched the register and the alt text but not the specimen generator's draw list. Caught by Checking what would happen to plan_sq05.png before declaring the slide done..
  • The first fix for that overwrite stopped drawing sq01 and sq02 entirely. Guarding on map_status: real. sq01 and sq02 are real, are drawn in that same file, and write to w02_* names - so the file was not the file. Caught by Reading the list of figures the script said it had written..

What to take from it

  • A dashboard is not a data source, but it is usually sitting on one. Appending .csv to a Tableau view path returns the records behind the picture; the equivalent for Power BI is a query endpoint. Learn the two and half of "the data is not downloadable" disappears.
  • When the obvious ranking variable is unpublished, do not rank on it anyway with the rows you happen to have. Rank on something measurable and say what it does and does not claim. Proximity is not movement.
  • You can geocode without a geocoder. Two published place files located 1,653 of 1,668 facilities. What matters is recording WHICH pass found each one, so a reader can discard the weak matches.
  • Never guess a coordinate. Two American markets and thirteen Mexican ones are left blank, with the reason written down, because an empty city field matching three different Ohio towns is a coin toss printed as a fact.
  • Two facility registers from two countries are two instruments, not one measure. Draw them in different SHAPES as well as different colors, so the distinction survives grayscale and a bad projector.
  • When a fill cannot reach 3:1 against its background, the outline has to. Test it after the projector wash, not before: gold squares that measured fine on the monitor fell to 2.8:1 washed out.
  • Promotion is a multi-file act. A plan that becomes real must be removed from whatever still draws its specimen, or the specimen wins the next time somebody runs the generator.

What this means for the job you are about to apply for

The client asked where the aggregation points are. The useful answer was that the American register runs out a hundred and thirty miles short of the border while the Mexican one runs right up to the river, that every port in the phased reopening has an approved market sitting on it, and that neither list is a census of where animals actually mix. Reporting the shape of the published record, and being explicit about where it stops, is worth more than a confident map of it.
