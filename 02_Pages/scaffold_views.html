Which of these is worth your time

View 1 is the one to present. It is the only view that shows both where an outcome goes quiet and how much sits in each week, and it holds all six outcomes at once. View 5 is the one to act on, because a chain that does not climb is a fixable problem and the others only show symptoms of it. View 2 makes a single point well — everything peaks early — and having made it, the six panels say the same thing six times. View 4 is for planning one week. View 3 is the shape you asked for and the least trustworthy of the five: the eye reads polygon area, which is the square of the number being encoded, so a share twice as large looks four times as large.
