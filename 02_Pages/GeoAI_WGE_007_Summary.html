What the data turned out to be

Every source that was tried, including the ones that gave nothing. A source that yielded nothing is a finding, not a gap in the record.

What the data turned out to be
SourceWhat it gave
FAO Gridded Livestock of the World v4, cattle, 2015gave what was needed — The only layer that lets a Mexican cell and a Texan cell be compared - one method applied to both countries at 5 arcmin. Four rasters, of which 8_Areakm.tif is the one an unwary analyst skips: the grid is in degrees, so pixel area falls with latitude, and dividing head by a constant area would bake a latitude gradient into the density map.
USDA NASS Census of Agriculture 2022, county levelgave what was needed — 3,060 county rows, and the three different readings of "important" that move the answer five hundred miles. No API key needed by this route, which is easy to miss: the whole Quick Stats corpus is published as gzipped pipe files.
SIAP, Cierre de la Produccion Pecuaria 2025, municipalgave part of it — 33,724 rows across 2,287 municipios - but PRODUCTION, not inventory. The download link carries no .csv in its href and two clicks did nothing; the page's own markup gave an opaque endpoint keyed by year.

What does not exist

  • A Mexican municipal INVENTORY series to pair with the US inventory census. SIAP's municipal open data is the production cierre; the poblacion ganadera series is an interactive report, not a municipal download. So the US census reports a stock and the Mexican one a flow, in different years, and the two are never joined - which is why the harmonized grid is the primary layer.

What was NOT built, and why

Read this section first if you read nothing else. The deliverable that was refused is the one you would most likely have produced.

  • A single cross-border choropleth of the two national censuses. NASS reports inventory in 2022 and the Mexican cierre reports production and head slaughtered in 2025. Coloring one join across the river would be comparing a stock to a flow and calling it a map.

What no check would have caught

The rules and gates this course runs on, and why each one would have passed the version that was wrong.

  • any checker here - a map with the wrong extent renders perfectly
  • the figure itself, for a density scale wrong by a constant factor - every value moves together and the map looks identical

What went wrong while building it

  • The first frame cut off the three counties holding the most cattle in Texas. An extent stopping at 33.5 degrees north; Deaf Smith, Castro and Hartley sit above 34.5. Caught by Looking at the render. No checker would have said a word..
  • A false explanation of an 8 percent bug was nearly written into the source as a comment. Believing a calibration that had been run under different conditions from the thing it calibrated. Caught by Re-running the calibration over the real data extent before committing the comment..

What to take from it

  • A choropleth of head by county is a map of how big the counties are. Units across this region differ in area by more than an order of magnitude.
  • Bin on PROJECTED coordinates in an equal-area projection. Binning in degrees makes the northern cells smaller and puts a latitude gradient into the answer.
  • Test a modeled surface against a source it was not built from before drawing it. GLW 2015 gives Texas 12,019,818 head against the 2022 census's 12,543,300 - four percent apart across a seven-year gap.
  • Two censuses that measure a stock and a flow, in different years, on boundaries that do not meet, are not joined. They name places and check the grid; the grid carries the pattern.
  • "Important" is a word that needs a measure attached. All cattle points at the Panhandle feedlots; the beef cow herd points at East and Central Texas. Same census, same year.
  • A check run under different conditions from the artifact reports phantom failures, exactly as a check sharing the artifact's assumptions reports phantom successes. Both are the same mistake with opposite sign.
  • Keep the source of a filter. Four megabytes of extract read nicely, but a filter whose 309 MB input has been deleted cannot be re-run or argued with.

What this means for the job you are about to apply for

The client asked where the cattle are. The useful answer was that the question has three answers depending on what "important" means, that neither country keeps its herd near the border, and that the risk therefore sits on the route rather than at either end. Being able to say "your question is underspecified, here is why, and here is what I think you actually want to know" is the difference between a supplier and an advisor.
