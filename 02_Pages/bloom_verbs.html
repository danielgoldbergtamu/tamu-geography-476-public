The master Bloom verb list

14 lists were captured from 12 independent offices — university teaching centers, assessment offices and faculty senates. Together they use 340 distinct verbs. Each verb below is placed where those lists agree it belongs, and the places they do not agree are listed too, because those are the words that look most useful and are least usable.

What the lists agree on

105 verbs are placed identically by every list that mentions them. Another 68 have a clear majority. The first line under each level is what this course matches on; the grayed line is agreed by the sources but not used here, either because no block in the course uses the word or because it is held back (below).

What they do not agree on

39 verbs are placed at two or more levels with no majority. These cannot carry a level, and they include the most-used words in the whole taxonomy. Twelve offices place relate at all six levels between them. This course matches on none of them.

Held back on purpose

These have a clear published verdict and this course still refuses them. Some name what a student's body does rather than what their mind does; some appear in nearly every instruction here at every level; three were adopted and then withdrawn after measuring what they did — change sent ten blocks to Create off the phrase "what would you change?", and every use of plan was the noun. The reason for each is in tools/bloom_verb_consensus.py.

build, change, complete, draw, find, give, meet, observe, perform, plan, point, prepare, produce, question, read, record, relate, review, set up, show, study, tell, test, use, write

Where these came from

Two taxonomies are in circulation and they are not the same. The 1956 original ends Synthesis, then Evaluation. The 2001 revision ends Evaluate, then Create — the top two levels are in the opposite order, so a verb can be the highest level in one scheme and the second-highest in the other. Some lists mix the two namings. Two of the 14 are republications of another list and are folded into one vote.
