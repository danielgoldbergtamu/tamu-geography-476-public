The three kinds of thing in this course

Engagements are the weekly check that the work of the session got done by you. Each one asks for a short account of what you produced and what review you gave. Writing and speaking assignments are the W-course requirements — drafts and finals, and the draft is graded. Project work is what your team owes the client, and it is the largest part of the grade. Attendance is separate from all three: attendance is being in the room, engagement is whether the work got done.

If you miss a deadline

Tell the instructor before it passes rather than after. Late work is accepted at the penalty in the syllabus; work that is late and unexplained costs you more than the points, because on a team project other people are waiting on it.

If this page and an assignment page disagree

Open the assignment itself and follow that, then say something. Each assignment has its own page with the full instructions and the rubric it is graded against; this page is the index, not the instructions.
