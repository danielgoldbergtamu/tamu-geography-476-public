Required length: 1 page

This is 1 page — and it must fill the page. Spilling onto a second page is a miss; so is handing in a half-empty one.

Page standard: 11 point serif, 1 inch margins, single spaced. Images, tables, captions and references do not count toward the page count; everything you wrote does.

Hitting a target exactly is not a formatting chore. It is the skill. Anyone can say something in unlimited space. Saying it in the space you were given — and no more — is what professional writing is, and it is what every employer, editor, reviewer and senior official you will ever write for requires of you.

Both directions are failures, and they fail differently. Running over means you did not decide what mattered. Running short means you did not have enough to say, or you stopped before finding it — and padding to reach the target is worse than either, because it wastes the reader’s attention on purpose.

On using AI here. AI will pad to a word count without complaint and it will do it well enough that you may not notice. Getting longer is not the problem you have. Ask it to cut instead: give it your draft and the target, tell it to remove the weakest fifteen percent, and then argue with what it chose. It will try to cut things that matter. Deciding what survives is the part that is actually yours.

AI use required on this assignment

Using AI on this assignment is required, not optional. You are being assessed on how well you direct and evaluate the tool — the skill employers now assume you have.

  1. Write your own first draft. This is a W assignment and the credit is for your writing, so the words you submit have to be yours. Use AI freely on everything around the draft - finding sources, checking a claim, arguing with what you have written - but not to produce the draft itself.
  2. Open the conversation by saying who you are and what the rule is. Paste this before anything else: "I am a student at Texas A&M University and I am bound by the Aggie Honor Code. This is a W course writing assignment and all the words I submit must be my own. For this assignment I may NOT use AI to generate words that I turn in. I am required to save and submit the transcript of this conversation with my assignment. Restate those rules back to me in your own words before we start." Asking it to restate rather than to agree is the point: a model that has misread the rule shows you on the first turn.
  3. Submit the transcript. Save the whole conversation and hand it in with the assignment. It is not a formality and it is not only for catching people - it is the evidence behind the AI Review Log, and where the log says what you rejected, the transcript shows what you were actually offered.
  4. Start from your draft and the feedback on it — from me, from your peers, and from your AI reviewers.
  5. Run the reviewer personas again on the revised version. A persona that found nothing new means you have either fixed everything or not pushed hard enough.
  6. Revise, then identify at least two critiques you reject with reasons.
  7. Complete the AI Review Log. On a final version the changes made field should be substantial — if it is thin, the draft feedback was not used.

Top critiquesThe three strongest critiques each persona raised.

The “critiques rejected” field carries the most weight. Accepting every suggestion an AI makes is not review — it is transcription. Show us where you overruled it, and why you were right to.
