The assignment

Build a professional portfolio website. It is the thing an employer looks at after your resume gets past the screen, and for most geospatial roles it matters more than the resume does.

What makes this work

  • A visitor knows within thirty seconds what you do well and what work you want.
  • Each piece is framed: what the problem was, what you did, what the outcome was. Artifacts without that framing tell a visitor nothing.
  • In team work, your own contribution is unambiguous.
  • Technical choices are explained, not just displayed.
  • It is accessible: navigable, works on a phone, images have alt text, adequate contrast. You are studying accessibility in this course; your own site should pass.

What to submit

  1. A link to your live portfolio, entered in Canvas.
  2. Your completed AI Review Log.

What happens next

Feedback returns within one week. The Final is graded primarily on how substantively you responded to it.

AI use required on this assignment

Using AI on this assignment is required, not optional. You are being assessed on how well you direct and evaluate the tool — the skill employers now assume you have.

  1. Write your own first draft. This is a W course and the credit is for your writing, so the words you submit have to be yours. Use AI freely on everything around the draft — finding sources, checking a claim, arguing with what you have written — but not to produce the draft itself. The full list of what AI may and may not do is on the AI Usage in this Class page, under "What AI may and may not do, item by item".
  2. Run your draft past all the reviewer personas listed for this assignment, using the prompts in the AI Prompt Library.
  3. Revise in response to the critiques you find persuasive.
  4. Deliberately identify at least two critiques you reject, and be able to explain why the reviewer was wrong.
  5. Complete the AI Review Log and attach it.

Required submission: AI Review Log

Attach a completed AI Review Log to your submission. Same five fields every time.

Required fields of the AI Review Log
Tool(s) usedWhich AI tool(s), e.g. TAMU AI Chat (Claude Sonnet), M365 Copilot.
Personas runWhich reviewer personas you used, and the prompt you gave each.
Top critiquesThe three strongest critiques each persona raised.
Changes madeWhat you actually changed in response, and where.
Critiques rejectedWhat you chose not to act on, and why.

The “critiques rejected” field carries the most weight. Accepting every suggestion an AI makes is not review — it is transcription. Show us where you overruled it, and why you were right to.

Before you submit, check your own

These used to be the questions a classmate would ask you. Ask them of your own draft instead, honestly, while there is still time to act on the answers. An AI reviewer will ask them too if you tell it to.

  • Thirty seconds only. What did you learn about this person, and would you keep looking?
  • Which piece would you click into, and why that one?
  • What did you have to hunt for that should have been obvious?
  • Is any claimed skill unsupported by anything on the site?
